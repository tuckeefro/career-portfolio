# Pouch Factory: production startup

**Professional work · Manufacturing systems and equipment integration**

When I arrived, the building was there, but the production infrastructure was not. My work was building the operation from that starting point: facility layout, compressed air, production machinery, operating procedures, and operator training.

**Head of Technical Operations · January–September 2024 (approximate)**

## My role

My work was split between technical planning and hands-on production work. I created working documents and safety plans, coordinated with outside contacts on certification requirements, produced CAD designs, and ran engineering calculations. I also built, operated, and worked on the production line.

That combination meant carrying engineering decisions into the physical setup and dealing with problems that appeared during assembly and operation.

My responsibility also involved stakeholder coordination: working with people who could help resolve technical questions and certification requirements as the buildout progressed.

## Featured engineering example

[Compressed-air system: requirements, equipment selection, and integration](production-compressed-air.md).

The air-system case covers sizing from machine requirements, allowance for a second line, rental versus purchase, and first-line operation without an additional receiver. It sits within the wider layout, production-line, documentation, and coordination work.

## Work I handled

- CAD, engineering calculations, facility layout, and site plans, with attention to material flow and equipment service access.
- P&IDs and compressed-air sizing from machine requirements, with allowance for a planned second line; compressor, filtration, and distribution integration.
- Production equipment integration and electromechanical/control troubleshooting.
- Temperature and humidity monitoring to investigate product-stability risks.
- Working documents, safety plans, certification coordination, and operator training.
- Hands-on production-line build, operation, and troubleshooting; interim workflows during equipment delays.
- Traceability and product-condition monitoring during startup.

## Startup conditions and stage reached

The buildout had a tight budget, an aggressive schedule, and a small crew. Equipment was arriving while facility work was still underway. I worked through unknowns as they appeared and adapted the setup to get the equipment running.

The project reached equipment break-in and an initial production run while the facility buildout remained unfinished. This account covers that startup stage; it does not claim a sustained production ramp or a fully commissioned facility.

The engineering challenge was getting machinery, utilities, and operating workflows to fit together as the project changed.

This work brought together mechanical engineering, equipment troubleshooting, and practical technical operations.
