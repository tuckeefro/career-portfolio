# Mines capstone: wildfire hotspot-detection UAV

**University team project · Mechanical engineering · 2021**

Our Colorado School of Mines team developed a four-rotor UAV prototype with a visual/thermal camera payload for wildfire situational awareness. The project focused on the needs of smaller fire districts: affordability, practical deployment, and ease of operation.

## Team project scope

- Stakeholder research with local fire personnel to define requirements.
- A quadrotor platform and camera-payload integration.
- Calculations and test planning for search/scan requirements and failure modes.
- Prototype construction and a final capstone presentation.

My role was mechanical engineering on the team. The team received overall second place in the capstone design challenge.
