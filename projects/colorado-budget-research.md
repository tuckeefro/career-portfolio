# Colorado budget research: rules, funding, and service

**Independent analysis · Public-data models and source reconciliation**

This work examines how Colorado funding rules and public records connect to allocations and services. Selected work includes a school-funding formula model, a transit award/service crosswalk, fiscal-note reconciliation, and a childcare funding-transition baseline.

## Analytical work

- Translating funding rules into a model that can be inspected.
- Reconciling definitions, periods, and units across public sources.
- Linking awards and scheduled service while preserving the distinction between scheduled and actually operated service.
- Recording where available data support a descriptive finding and where they cannot establish a causal effect.

The work combines quantitative analysis with judgment about what the records can measure. A selected case study includes the route-level transcription, arithmetic-checking script, and a dashboard observation with source and measurement limits. It traces a public transit grant to announced schedule changes; it does not infer operated service, grant spending by route, or causal effects.
