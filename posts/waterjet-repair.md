## Post copy

Three working days from shutdown to resumed production after a waterjet pump rebuild.

The problem showed up in the cut: the machine would cut cleanly through the material, then remove surface material for roughly an inch without penetrating. The inconsistent cuts were disrupting production.

I investigated the high-pressure pump and found more mechanical play than expected in its cylinder assemblies. I carried out the scheduled 5,000-hour rebuild using the standard kit: disassembly, inspection, replacement of the covered internal components, reassembly, and machine checks.

Consistent through-cutting returned, and production restarted.

The figure traces the cutting symptom, inspection, rebuild, and return to production. It is a reconstruction from my own repair account.
